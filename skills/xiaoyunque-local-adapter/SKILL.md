# Xiaoyunque local adapter

Require PASS pre-generation QA and a capability-compatible contract. Use the installed Xiaoyunque capability only for provider syntax and local execution; it cannot rewrite the upstream production decision.

Keep credentials in the local provider tool or local environment and out of Git, contracts, prompts, logs, and evidence. Bind every uploaded asset to its approved role and scope. Store only sanitized payload/hash, task ID, cost, status and local output path.

One authorization permits only its stated submission. Unknown state, retry, provider/model switch, additional asset generation, duration/cost expansion, publishing, or overwrite must stop for new authority.
